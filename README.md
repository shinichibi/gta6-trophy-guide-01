VICE LEDGER is an offline GTA VI trophy guide that runs entirely in your browser: a searchable checklist of 51 trophies, every one with its own step-by-step guide, missable warnings and time estimates. It tracks your progress, points and hours remaining, suggests your next best trophy, and lays out a six-phase Platinum Roadmap generated from the trophy data — no account, no network, your progress never leaves your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
